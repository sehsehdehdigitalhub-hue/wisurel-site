Privacy

Draft — not a legal document. This page describes how the app handles information. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be reviewed before it is relied on.

What is stored

Receipt and document files you upload, the records extracted from them, staff names and WhatsApp numbers you register, report recipients, and account details needed to sign in.

Where it is stored

In demo mode, in the browser on the device you use. In a connected workspace, in your Supabase project, with row-level security limiting each workspace to its own records.

Third parties

Documents you process are sent to the AI provider you have configured (Anthropic) to be read. WhatsApp messages go through Meta. Email reports go through Resend if you enable it. Each provider's own privacy terms also apply.

Retention and deletion

Records stay until removed. Deleting a record in the app does not yet delete its stored source file.

Contact

Contact details are to be added by Wisurel.
